Our City Is Warming. It's Time to Act.

🌡️

Rising Temperatures

Ahilyanagar records temperatures exceeding 42°C in summer. Urban heat islands form where green cover disappears. Each tree can cool its surroundings by up to 2–8°C.

🌫️

Shrinking Green Cover

Rapid urbanization has reduced Ahilyanagar's green area significantly over the last two decades. We need active reforestation to restore ecological balance.

💧

Groundwater Depletion

Trees act as natural water reservoirs. Their root systems recharge groundwater and prevent soil erosion, directly benefiting agriculture and daily water supply.

About Green City Mission

About Green City Mission

The Green City Mission (ग्रीन सिटी मोहीम) was launched on May 1, 2026, under the visionary leadership of Deputy Mayor Adv. Dhananjay Krishnabhau Jadhav, in partnership with Ahilyanagar Mahanagar Palika and Sai Dwarka Seva Trust.

The mission aims to plant 1 lakh (100,000) trees across Ahilyanagar within one year — making it cleaner, greener, and more liveable for every citizen.

Organizations, schools, corporates, and individuals are invited to contribute through tree plantation, tree guards, saplings, or maintenance support under their CSR or personal initiative.

🌳

Goal: 1,00,000 Trees

Aiming to restore green cover across Ahilyanagar.

📅

Duration: 1 Year (May 2026 – May 2027)

A year-long campaign for sustainable growth.

🏙️

City: Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra

A local mission rooted in civic pride and care.
